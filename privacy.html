Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026. MayhemNull is a free productivity tracker made by Mridul Saravanan.

If you do not sign in

Everything you track stays in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub or Discord. We receive only your name, your email address and your profile picture from that service, and we never see your password.

  • Your tracking data (tasks, notes, times, settings) is saved to your account so you can use it on other devices. It is stored with Google Firebase.
  • Pictures you add as a wallpaper are never uploaded. They stay in your browser on that device.
  • Your account is identified by your email address, so signing in with a different service that uses the same verified email opens the same data.
  • Only you can read or change your data. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Music

If you play music, the Spotify or YouTube player is loaded from Spotify or YouTube, and their own privacy policies apply to it. YouTube searches go through our server to YouTube; we do not keep a record of what you searched.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time and choose to remove the data from your browser. To delete the copy in your account, email mridul8527@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 7 days.
